THE MAIN BOARD

Trip Plan

SEASON FITRecommended

Late May is the sweet spot for this route: cruise season is underway, whale watching is strong, the summer trains normally operate, and Denali bus access begins around May 20.

Best target window: May 19–31, 2028

This lean route removes the Seward day trip and the dedicated rest day. Denali stays late in the trip, when park-bus and summer-train service are more likely to be operating. Confirm all final 2028 schedules before booking non-refundable connections.

13-DAY FRAMEWORK

Itinerary

A compact cruise-and-land route that keeps Glacier Bay, White Pass, whale watching and Denali while cutting two expensive land days.

  1. 01
    Vancouver arrival

    Arrive one night before sailing to protect the cruise connection.

  2. 02–08
    Inside Passage cruise

    Board in Vancouver for Ship Life, Ketchikan, Juneau with Mendenhall and whales, Skagway with White Pass Railway, Glacier Bay, then Whittier.

  3. 09
    Whittier to Anchorage

    Take the direct motorcoach after disembarkation and stay near the rail depot.

  4. 10
    Denali Star northbound

    Ride Adventure Class to Denali and use the shared Vista Dome when seats are available. GoldStar is an optional paid upgrade for a reserved dome seat.

  5. 11
    Denali National Park

    Take the lower-cost transit bus toward East Fork and watch for moose, grizzly bears and mountain views.

  6. 12
    Return to Anchorage

    Use the motorcoach instead of a second premium train journey, then stay one final night.

  7. 13
    Fly home

    Leave Anchorage on an open-jaw international ticket, avoiding a return trip to Vancouver.
