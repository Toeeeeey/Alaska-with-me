NATIONAL PARK

Glacier Bay

A living landscape carved by ice, tide, and time.

Glacier Bay is the defining Alaska day: immense tidewater glaciers, snow-striped peaks, and water so still it can hold the whole sky.

THE EXPERIENCE

What makes it special

Park rangers often board visiting ships to share the science and stories of this protected place. Watch quietly for calving ice, listen for the deep crack before it falls, and give the scale time to sink in.

PLAN AHEAD

Know before you go

  • Best viewingOpen deck with a wide field of view
  • PackGloves, rain shell, and a warm drink
  • Look forCalving ice, harbor seals, and mountain goats
