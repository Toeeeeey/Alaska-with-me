Trip Plan

ROUTE FITEasy & focused

One international gateway, one round-trip cruise, and no Alaska land transfer. Late May keeps Glacier Bay, whale watching, White Pass, and Ketchikan in one compact journey.

Target window: May 26–June 4, 2028

Fly into Seattle one day before the Royal Princess sailing on May 27. The ship returns to Seattle on June 3, leaving one city day before the flight home.

10-DAY FRAMEWORK

Itinerary

A relaxed Seattle round trip with a seven-night balcony cruise and no difficult overland extension.

  1. MAY 26
    Arrive in Seattle

    Check in near downtown, recover from the flight, and protect the cruise connection with a pre-cruise night.

  2. MAY 27–28
    Embarkation & sea day

    Board Royal Princess, settle into the balcony cabin, and sail north from Seattle.

  3. MAY 29
    Juneau

    Combine humpback whale watching with Mendenhall Glacier during the 13:00–21:15 port call.

  4. MAY 30
    Skagway

    Ride the White Pass Summit excursion during the long 06:00–20:30 port day.

  5. MAY 31
    Glacier Bay

    Enjoy ranger-led scenic cruising from the ship; no additional excursion is required.

  6. JUN 01–02
    Ketchikan & Victoria

    Walk Creek Street in Ketchikan, then make a short late-evening call in Victoria.

  7. JUN 03–04
    Seattle & flight home

    Disembark at the same port, enjoy Pike Place Market and the waterfront, then fly home the next day.
