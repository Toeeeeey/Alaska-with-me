CORE HIGHLIGHT · SKAGWAY

White Pass Railway

Ride from tidewater into the mountains on a gold-rush engineering marvel.

The narrow-gauge White Pass & Yukon Route climbs steeply from Skagway past waterfalls, gorges, trestles, and traces of the Klondike Trail.

THE EXPERIENCE

What makes it special

Sit on the left leaving Skagway for many of the early valley views, though both sides deliver drama. Step onto the platform between cars when permitted for fresh air and unobstructed photographs.
