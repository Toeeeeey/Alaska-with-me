WILDLIFE

Grizzly Bear

Power, patience, and the true scale of Alaska wilderness.

Seeing a grizzly in the wild is unforgettable precisely because it is wild. Alaska's open landscapes can offer extraordinary views without compromising the animal's space.

THE EXPERIENCE

What makes it special

Follow every local instruction, carry bear spray where advised, and never approach or feed a bear. On guided viewing trips, calm observation and distance create the safest, most rewarding experience.

PLAN AHEAD

Know before you go

  • Best practiceTravel with an experienced local guide
  • NeverApproach, feed, or block a bear's path
  • Look forCoastal meadows, rivers, and open tundra
