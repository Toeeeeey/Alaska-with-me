NATIONAL PARK

Denali

Six million acres of open tundra beneath North America's highest summit.

Denali National Park is vast, quiet, and wonderfully indifferent to schedules. The mountain may appear for minutes or stay behind clouds all day; the wilderness is the reward either way.

THE EXPERIENCE

What makes it special

Use the park bus system to travel deeper along the single road, scanning wide valleys rather than waiting for animals beside the road. Carry layers, water, and patience.

PLAN AHEAD

Know before you go

  • Summit20,310 feet / 6,190 metres
  • LandscapeTundra, taiga, glaciers, and braided rivers
  • Look forDall sheep, caribou, wolves, and bears
