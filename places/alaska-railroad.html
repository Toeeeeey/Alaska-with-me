SCENIC RAIL

Alaska Railroad

Big windows, long valleys, and wilderness far beyond the road.

The Alaska Railroad connects coast and interior through some of the state's grandest scenery, turning travel time into a highlight of the trip.

THE EXPERIENCE

What makes it special

The Coastal Classic and Denali Star routes reveal different sides of Alaska, from Turnagain Arm to broad interior rivers. A dome car makes the landscape feel almost cinematic.

PLAN AHEAD

Know before you go

  • Classic routesCoastal Classic and Denali Star
  • Best seatUpper-level dome when available
  • Look forMoose, braided rivers, and distant peaks
