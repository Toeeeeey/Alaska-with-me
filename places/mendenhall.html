JUNEAU

Mendenhall

A glacier experience just beyond Alaska's capital.

Mendenhall Glacier flows from the vast Juneau Icefield into a lake framed by mountains and temperate rainforest.

THE EXPERIENCE

What makes it special

Follow the short trail to Nugget Falls for the classic meeting of waterfall and glacier, or explore quieter forest paths nearby. Conditions change quickly, so bring rain protection even on a bright morning.

PLAN AHEAD

Know before you go

  • DistanceAbout 13 miles from downtown Juneau
  • Easy walkNugget Falls Trail
  • Look forIcebergs, waterfalls, and black bears
