INSIDE PASSAGE

Ketchikan

A colorful port between rainforest and sea.

Ketchikan layers working waterfront, Indigenous art, wooden boardwalks, and deep-green Tongass rainforest into one wonderfully walkable stop.

THE EXPERIENCE

What makes it special

Begin along historic Creek Street, then make time for the region's Tlingit, Haida, and Tsimshian cultural heritage. Rain is part of the atmosphere here—wear a good shell and keep exploring.

PLAN AHEAD

Know before you go

  • Known forSalmon, totem art, and rainforest
  • WalkCreek Street and the waterfront
  • PackWaterproof shoes and a light rain jacket
