WILDLIFE

Humpback Whales

A flash of white water, a tail in the air, then silence.

Every sighting is unscripted. Humpbacks return to Alaska's rich summer feeding grounds, surfacing in channels and bays with blows, flukes, and sometimes spectacular breaches.

THE EXPERIENCE

What makes it special

Choose a small-boat excursion for a lower viewpoint and fewer passengers. Keep your camera ready, but spend time simply watching the whale's rhythm—it often hints at where it may surface next.
