WILDLIFE

Moose

Alaska's long-legged giant is built for the northern wild.

A moose can appear surprisingly quietly at a forest edge, in a pond, or beside a road. Its calm silhouette hides remarkable size and strength.

THE EXPERIENCE

What makes it special

Give every moose generous space, especially cows with calves and bulls during autumn. Use a long lens, never approach for a photograph, and retreat if its ears flatten or it changes direction toward you.

PLAN AHEAD

Know before you go

  • Where to scanWetlands, willow flats, and forest edges
  • Most activeEarly morning and evening
  • SafetyStay far away and keep an escape route
