START & FINISH

Seattle

One gateway makes the whole journey easier.

Seattle turns this Alaska plan into a simple round trip: arrive, cruise, return to the same city, and fly home without an overland transfer or a second domestic flight.

THE EXPERIENCE

A relaxed city bookend

Stay near downtown for easy access to Pike Place Market, the waterfront, and the cruise terminal. The final city day creates a calm finish and avoids rushing from the ship directly to an international flight.
