THE MAIN BOARD

May 2028
Trip Plan

SEASON FITRecommended

Late May is the sweet spot for this route: cruise season is underway, whale watching is strong, the summer trains normally operate, and Denali bus access begins around May 20.

Best target window: May 17–31, 2028

Keep Denali after May 20, allow one flexible weather day, and confirm the final 2028 cruise, rail, and park-bus schedules before booking non-refundable connections.

15-DAY FRAMEWORK

Itinerary

The order protects the weather-sensitive land days and keeps every highlight from the original wish list.

  1. 01–02
    Vancouver & embarkation

    Arrive one night early, recover from the flight, then board the Alaska cruise.

  2. 03–09
    Inside Passage cruise

    Ship Life, Ketchikan, Juneau with Mendenhall and whales, Skagway with White Pass Railway, Glacier Bay, then Whittier.

  3. 10
    Anchorage reset day

    Transfer from Whittier, do laundry, rest, and keep this day as a weather buffer.

  4. 11
    Seward by glass-dome train

    Ride the Coastal Classic, add a Resurrection Bay or Kenai Fjords cruise, and return to Anchorage.

  5. 12–14
    Denali National Park

    Take the Denali Star north, ride the park bus after May 20, and watch for moose and grizzly bears.

  6. 15
    Return to Anchorage

    Travel south, stay flexible for the evening, and take the international flight home.
